# 🧠 rizzo-flow - Make AI Decisions, Right on Your PC

## 🚀 What Is rizzo-flow?

rizzo-flow is a simple, private, and free program that lets you use AI to help make decisions - without sending your information to the cloud or paying for expensive subscriptions. Think of it as a smart assistant that runs entirely on your computer. It takes a type of AI called an "LLM" (Large Language Model) and uses it to give you clear, structured answers to your questions, but it does all the work locally - meaning your data never leaves your machine.

## ⚡ Why You'll Love It

- **100% Private:** Your questions and data stay on your computer. No internet connection is needed after installation.
- **No Token Fees:** Many AI tools charge you per "token" (a piece of text). rizzo-flow doesn't generate any tokens, so it's completely free to use.
- **Simple Interface:** If you can use a web browser, you can use rizzo-flow. No command lines, no coding, no technical knowledge required.
- **Fast and Lightweight:** It runs efficiently on most modern Windows computers without slowing things down.
